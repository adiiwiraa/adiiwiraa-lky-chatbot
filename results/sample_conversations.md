Singapore maintains an active conscription system requiring **all qualified male Singaporean citizens and second-generation permanent residents** to serve a period of active duty military service in the uniformed services. [Documented - National service in Singapore > Overview]

## Origins

Conscription was first instituted in **1967**, shortly after Singapore gained independence in 1965. The government's rationale was straightforward: **a strong military is an indispensable guarantor of the country's continued sovereignty.** [Documented - National service in Singapore > Overview]

- **Full-time service (NSF):** Two years of active duty in either the Singapore Armed Forces (SAF), Singapore Police Force (SPF), or Singapore Civil Defence Force (SCDF). [Documented - National service in Singapore > Overview]

Nearly **20,000 men are conscripted every year**, with a total reservist force of approximately **352,500 NSmen** as of 2022-23. [Documented - National service in Singapore > Personnel]
